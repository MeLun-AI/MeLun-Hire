# MeLun — Complete Brand Assets & Usage Guide

This document is the source-of-truth guide for the logo, monogram, wordmark, favicon, colour palette, and usage rules included in this package.

## 1. Brand identity

MeLun's visual identity is built around a distinctive **M/L monogram with a leaf**, paired with the **MeLun wordmark**.

- The monogram combines the letterforms **M** and **L**.
- The leaf is part of the approved symbol.
- The orange L is a solid letterform. **Do not add an orange fruit or melon slice inside the L.**
- Use the supplied artwork files; do not redraw, distort, or reconstruct the mark.

| Warm orange | `#FF7418` | Orange L / primary warm accent |
| Golden yellow | `#FFB91F` | Orange gradient highlight |

### Colour guidance
- Preserve the supplied green-to-orange relationship in the full-colour mark.
- Use white or black monochrome assets when a single-colour treatment is required.
- Do not independently recolour the logo artwork unless a new approved brand version is created.
- For exact digital colour matching, sample the approved master artwork and confirm with the brand owner before changing the palette.

## 5. Background and contrast selection

| Background | Preferred asset |
|---|---|
| White / light neutral | Full-colour mark or black monochrome |
| Dark green / charcoal / dark neutral | White monochrome, or full-colour dark-background variant after contrast check |
| Coloured background | White monochrome if the full-colour mark does not have sufficient contrast |
| One-colour printing | Black or white monochrome, according to the substrate |

Transparent PNG files should be placed directly over the intended background. The JPG and white-background PNG files include a background and should not be treated as transparent artwork.

## 6. Clear space, sizing, and placement

- Keep clear space around the entire logo, including the leaf.
- Preserve the original aspect ratio; scale proportionally only.
- Never stretch, squash, rotate, skew, crop, or add shadows/outlines to the logo.
- Do not separate the M, L, or leaf into independent decorative elements.
- Do not place text, borders, or other graphics over the mark.
- Keep the wordmark legible; avoid reducing it below a size where the lettering becomes unclear.
- Use the horizontal or stacked arrangement only when an approved corresponding asset is supplied. The combined lockup in this package is stacked.

## 7. Do not

- Do not add an orange fruit, melon slice, or circular fruit detail inside the L.
- Do not change the green/orange colours or invent new gradients.
- Do not use the black mark on a dark background or the white mark on a white background.
- Do not use a low-resolution favicon as a full-size logo.
- Do not recreate the wordmark using an arbitrary font.
- Do not modify the original artwork files in place; create a separately named version if an approved change is required.

## 8. Quick file selection

- **Website header on a light background:** `logo/MeLun_Logo_Mark.png` or the primary transparent logo.
- **Website header on a dark background:** `MeLun_Logo_Mark_White.png` or the dark-background full-colour variant after checking contrast.
- **Text-only placement:** `MeLun_Wordmark.png`.
- **White text-only placement:** `MeLun_Wordmark_White.png`.
- **Black text-only placement:** `MeLun_Wordmark_Black.png`.
- **Centered brand presentation:** `MeLun_Logo_And_Wordmark.png`.
- **Browser / PWA icon:** choose the matching size from `favicon/`.

## 9. Asset maintenance

Keep this README with the complete asset folder whenever the assets are shared with designers, developers, agencies, or print vendors. If a logo or colour is officially changed, update the artwork and this guide together, and retain the previous approved version in a clearly named archive folder.
